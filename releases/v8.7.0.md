# MobHunting 8.7.0

MobHunting 8.7.0 removes obsolete integrations and keeps stable identities for
historical data while targeting the maintained Paper 1.21.11 stack.

## Highlights

- Removes adapters for obsolete shops, holograms, disguises and mob plugins.
- Preserves historical database IDs so stored statistics are not reinterpreted.
- Isolates achievement progress by mob source, including InfernalMobs.
- Records rejected kill and assist penalties without reporting money that was not withdrawn.
- Retains CMILib as the maintained action-bar and boss-bar backend.
- Documents retained integrations that are not available in the reference environment.

## Compatibility

- Java 21.
- Paper 1.21.11 is the validated runtime.
- CustomItemsLib 1.3.0 is required.
- BagOfGold 4.7.0 is the validated physical-currency integration.

## Validation

- All automated regression tests passed.
- Startup and shutdown passed with and without BagOfGold.
- Vanilla and InfernalMobs achievements, kill rewards, physical pickup and CMILib messages were tested in game.
